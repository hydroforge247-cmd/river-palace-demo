While you wait for your food

Skip a stone across the Vaal.

Five buoys bob on the river, from close by to way out. Grab the stone, flick it low and fast, and hit them in order before your order arrives.

Grab the stone and flick it at buoy 1

Tip: angle your flick towards the buoy, and flick harder for the far ones. On a computer, drag the stone with the mouse and let go.
